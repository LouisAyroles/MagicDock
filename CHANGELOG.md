## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-10

### Added

- Native macOS menu bar interface.
- Magic Keyboard and Magic Mouse selection and status.
- Bonjour discovery and authenticated peer commands.
- Bluetooth release, pair, connect, verification, retry, and rollback workflow.
- Keychain-backed 256-bit pairing key.
- Launch-at-login support.
- Swift unit tests and reproducible app packaging script.
